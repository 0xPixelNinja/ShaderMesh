How renting works

Trust comes from availability, not from re-checking output. It is enforced by the contracts.

1

Lease and fund escrow

Pick a GPU and open a lease. Your payment is locked in escrow for the duration.

2

Provider proves uptime

The provider posts a signed heartbeat each interval and passes a hardware attestation.

3

Pay per interval

Escrow streams per verified interval. Unused time is refunded and downtime is slashed.

Or call a model directly

Skip the setup. Use a managed, OpenAI-compatible endpoint served by providers, billed per token from prepaid credits.
