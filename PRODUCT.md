# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ShaderMesh serves compute requesters, independent GPU and CPU providers,
deployment operators, and project reviewers. Requesters browse capacity, fund
leases, monitor service, and call managed models. Providers stake, publish
capacity, accept workloads, operate nodes, and monitor earnings and reputation.

## Product Purpose

ShaderMesh coordinates compute rental through Ethereum without placing compute
on chain. Smart contracts control escrow, stake, settlement, and penalties.
Untrusted off-chain services execute workloads, index events, and route requests.
Success means users can inspect the trust boundary and complete rental,
inference, and verifiable-task flows without surrendering custody of funds.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and credible. The interface should communicate technical
confidence without cryptocurrency hype or promotional language.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon cryptocurrency aesthetics, violet gradients, glassmorphism, large
glows, oversized marketing metrics, decorative card grids, pill-shaped primary
buttons, and claims that hide trust assumptions or prototype limitations.

## Design Principles

- Make value movement and transaction state explicit.
- Keep dense protocol information calm and scannable.
- Use established application conventions instead of decorative novelty.
- Distinguish on-chain guarantees from off-chain service claims.
- Provide actionable empty, loading, failure, and wallet states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states,
reduced motion, responsive layouts, and status communication that does not rely
on color alone.
